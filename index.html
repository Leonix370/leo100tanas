Leo 100 Tanas – personal uploader

This is a private, single-user tool. It renders a daily animated episode of the series “Leo, 100 Tanas in 30 days” on the creator's own computer and sends it to the creator's own TikTok account as a draft (inbox), using the TikTok Content Posting API. The creator reviews and publishes each draft manually in the TikTok app.
